# PAGE 2 — SOLUTIONS

Page intro: *SUME handles the mechanical and electrical scope that most facilities split across too many vendors. We bring the full picture — and the specialist depth each system requires.*

The page shows capabilities directly. Entity names appear naturally within service descriptions — never labeled as "partner," "subsidiary," or "group." The visitor sees one capable, well-connected company.

### 2.1 Power & Energy
When the power goes out, everything else stops. We build systems designed so that moment never comes — and keep them ready if it does.
- **Standby & Prime Generators** — Yuchai diesel and gas gensets, containerized and site-built, sized to actual facility load.
- **Solar PV Systems** — rooftop and ground-mount installations, delivered with Powerbrain; 50+ MWp installed across commercial and industrial sites.
- **Fuel Monitoring** — real-time tracking of fuel levels, consumption rates, and anomalies for generator reserves, powered by Ramus instrumentation.
- **Engine Maintenance & Overhaul** — authorized spare parts and overhaul for IHI / Niigata land and marine engines through Ramoco, backed by certified engineers.

### 2.2 Precision Cooling & HVAC
Cooling is where most of a facility's electricity goes. Getting it wrong costs money every month. We design and optimize systems that hold efficiency over the long run.
- **Chiller Plants & VRF Systems** — Midea, Broad, and Hisense for high-density and commercial cooling loads.
- **Chiller Optimization & Retrofit** — plant automation, VSD retrofits, pump rewinding. On one 3×550 TR plant, we took efficiency from 1.10 to 0.77 kW/TR.
- **Cooling-as-a-Service** — the client pays nothing upfront. Powerbrain finances, installs, and operates the cooling system; performance is contractually guaranteed and backed by an insurance-grade performance bond, measured against the IPMVP standard. Aligned with Indonesia's Permen ESDM 1/2026 ESCO framework.

### 2.3 Monitoring & Security
You cannot fix what you cannot see. We integrate instrumentation and surveillance into a single operational view of the facility.
- **Flow Metering** — Emerson and Endress+Hauser meters for chilled-water and process lines, integrated by Ramus.
- **Surveillance Systems** — KINGSAT solutions through Ramus. ⚠️ *Build note: KINGSAT's catalog is maritime VSAT/TVRO/marine CCTV — verify fit for building/DC security before emphasizing here. Kept per instruction.*
- **Smart Control & Monitoring** — centralized visibility across power, cooling, and fuel.

### 2.4 Integrated M&E Contracting
This is what SUME was built to do. One contractor, full scope — design, procurement, installation, commissioning, and maintenance. Clients deal with one team, sign one contract, and have one number to call when something needs attention.
