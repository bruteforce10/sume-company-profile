# PAGE 2 — SOLUTIONS

Page intro: *Engineered for environments where downtime is not an option. SUME delivers the full mechanical and electrical scope — drawing on a network of specialist capabilities that mission-critical facilities depend on.*

### 2.1 Power & Energy
The backbone of any critical facility. Primary and standby power engineered for continuous operation, with the monitoring and maintenance to keep it reliable across its full life.
- **Standby & Prime Generators** — Yuchai diesel & gas gensets; containerized and site-built configurations, sized to facility load.
- **Solar PV Systems** — rooftop and ground-mount design and installation, delivered with Powerbrain; `50+ MWp` installed across commercial and industrial sites.
- **Fuel Monitoring Systems** — real-time fuel level, consumption, and theft detection for generator fuel reserves, powered by Ramus instrumentation.
- **Engine Maintenance & Overhaul** — authorized spare parts and overhaul for IHI / Niigata land and marine engines through Ramoco, supported by certified engineers.

#### Aftersales Service (IHI / Niigata engines)
Reliability does not end at commissioning. Engine systems are backed by a structured aftersales program delivered through SUME's ecosystem and the manufacturer:
- **8 certified Indonesian engineers** trained in Niigata Power Systems, Japan.
- **20+ years** combined experience in maintenance, overhaul, and troubleshooting for both marine and land-use engines.
- **Dedicated Niigata engineers** from the manufacturer's Customer Support Center for the Indonesian region — troubleshooting, supervision, and overhaul works.
- **Annual courtesy visits** to each customer in Indonesia by the principal.
- **Open communication channel** between manufacturer and customer for any clarification or troubleshooting.
- **Case-by-case field reports** — photo details, findings, and manufacturer recommendations.

> Build note: Detailed aftersales content lives here on Solutions (under Power & Energy 2.1). Projects 5.4 ("Power & Engine Capability") stays concise as track-record proof — do not duplicate the full list in both places. Data Center page links here rather than repeating it. Framing per ecosystem rule: "engines we service / support," not partner/subsidiary wording.

### 2.2 Precision Cooling & HVAC
Cooling typically consumes 40–60% of a facility's electricity. We design and optimize for both performance and energy efficiency.
- **Chiller Plants & VRF Systems** — Midea, Broad, and Hisense systems for high-density and commercial loads.
- **Chiller Optimization & Retrofit** — plant automation, VSD retrofits, pump rewinding; measurable efficiency gains (e.g. 1.10 → 0.77 kW/TR on a 3×550 TR plant).
- **Cooling-as-a-Service** — zero-CAPEX cooling under a performance-guaranteed energy service agreement, delivered with Powerbrain; backed by an insurance-grade performance bond and IPMVP measurement & verification, aligned with Indonesia's Permen ESDM 1/2026 ESCO framework.

### 2.3 Monitoring & Security
Critical infrastructure requires constant visibility. We integrate instrumentation and surveillance into a unified operational view.
- **Flow Metering** — Emerson and Endress+Hauser meters for chilled-water and process monitoring, integrated by Ramus.
- **Surveillance Systems** — KINGSAT surveillance solutions through Ramus. ⚠️ *Build note: KINGSAT's catalog is maritime VSAT/TVRO/marine CCTV — verify fit for building/DC security before emphasizing here. Kept per instruction.*
- **Smart Control & Monitoring** — centralized monitoring of power, cooling, and fuel systems.

### 2.4 Integrated M&E Contracting
SUME's core. A single accountable contractor across the full mechanical and electrical scope — design, procurement, installation, commissioning, and ongoing maintenance. One partner, not a dozen vendors.
