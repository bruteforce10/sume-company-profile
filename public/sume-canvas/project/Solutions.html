01 — Power
Power & Energy

The backbone of any critical facility.

Primary and standby power engineered for continuous operation, with the monitoring and maintenance to keep it reliable across its full life.

  • Standby & Prime Generators

    Yuchai diesel & gas gensets; containerized and site-built configurations, sized to facility load.

  • Solar PV Systems

    Rooftop and ground-mount design and installation, delivered with Powerbrain; 50+ MWp installed.

  • Fuel Monitoring Systems

    Real-time fuel level, consumption, and theft detection, powered by Ramus instrumentation.

  • Engine Maintenance & Overhaul

    Authorized spare parts and overhaul for IHI / Niigata land and marine engines through Ramoco.

Aftersales Service — IHI / Niigata Engines

Reliability does not end at commissioning. Engine systems are backed by a structured aftersales program delivered with the manufacturer.

  • 8 certified Indonesian engineers trained in Niigata Power Systems, Japan.
  • 20+ years combined experience in maintenance, overhaul, and troubleshooting — marine and land-use engines.
  • Dedicated Niigata engineers from the Customer Support Center for the Indonesian region — troubleshooting, supervision, and overhaul.
  • Annual courtesy visits to each customer in Indonesia by the principal.
  • Open communication channel between manufacturer and customer for any clarification or troubleshooting.
  • Case-by-case field reports — photo details, findings, and manufacturer recommendations.
02 — Cooling
Precision Cooling & HVAC

Cooling that performs under load.

Cooling typically consumes 40–60% of a facility's electricity. We design and optimize for both performance and energy efficiency.

  • Chiller Plants & VRF Systems

    Midea, Broad, and Hisense systems for high-density and commercial loads.

  • Chiller Optimization & Retrofit

    Plant automation, VSD retrofits, pump rewinding; measurable gains (e.g. 1.10 → 0.77 kW/TR on a 3×550 TR plant).

  • Cooling-as-a-Service

    Zero-CAPEX cooling under a performance-guaranteed energy service agreement, delivered with Powerbrain; backed by an insurance-grade performance bond and IPMVP measurement & verification.
